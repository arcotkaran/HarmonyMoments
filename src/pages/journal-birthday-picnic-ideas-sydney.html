Birthday picnic ideas in Sydney.

A restaurant table for eight is fine. A styled picnic under the trees at golden hour is something people talk about afterwards. Here is how to put one together in Sydney without it becoming a second job.

  1. Keep the group smaller than you think.

    Picnics work beautifully up to about six people. Beyond that you lose the single-conversation feeling that makes them special, and setup becomes a logistical exercise. If you are hosting more, consider a longer table or splitting into two settings rather than one very long row.

  2. Late afternoon beats midday.

    Midday sun is harsh, hot and unflattering in photos. Arriving around two hours before sunset means you eat in beautiful light and finish in that lovely blue hour afterwards. It also means less competition for the good spots.

  3. Choose shade you can rely on.

    Sydney parks with mature trees — Centennial Park, Parsley Bay, the quieter parts of the Botanic Garden — give you dependable shade that moves slowly. Open lawns look wonderful in photos but can be brutal on a warm afternoon with no cover.

  4. Make one thing personal.

    You do not need a theme. One personal touch does more work than a fully co-ordinated palette — a favourite flower, a cake from the bakery they always mention, a photo propped against the centrepiece. It is the detail people notice and remember.

  5. Decide who packs down.

    This is the part that ruins otherwise perfect birthdays. Someone has to carry everything back to the car in the dark, usually the person whose birthday it is. If you are doing it yourself, plan for it. If you would rather not, that is exactly what we handle.
