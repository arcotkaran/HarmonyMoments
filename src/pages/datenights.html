Questions

How far in advance should I book?

A week or two is comfortable, and weekend sunset slots go first. If your date is sooner, message us anyway — we can often make it work.

Where in Sydney can you set up?

Harbourside headlands, beaches, quiet park corners, and your own backyard — right across Sydney. If you’re not sure where, we’ll suggest a spot that suits your timing and the light.

What if it rains?

We watch the forecast with you and move the date at no extra cost, or shift to an at-home setup so the evening still happens.

Can we stay as long as we like?

Your setup is yours for the booked window, and we’re flexible if you want longer — just let us know when you book.

Do you provide the food?

We offer personally curated catering — put together by hand for your occasion, never an off-the-shelf grazing box. It’s an optional add-on, arranged on request and quoted separately, so you’re just as welcome to bring your own.
