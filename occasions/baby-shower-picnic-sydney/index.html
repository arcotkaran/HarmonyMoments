Questions

How much does a baby shower picnic cost?

Baby shower setups start from $350 for a group of four to six, which covers the styled setting, florals and setup. Extras like a balloon arch, dessert styling, additional florals and curated grazing sit on top.

How many guests can you style for?

Up to six around the low table. Planning something larger? Message us with the numbers and we’ll tell you what’s possible.

Can we choose the colours?

Yes — tell us your palette and we’ll style the florals, linen and décor to match it.

Where in Sydney can you set up?

Harbourside lawns, quiet park corners, and your own backyard — right across Sydney.

What if it rains?

We watch the forecast with you and move the date at no extra cost, or shift to an at-home setup so the day still happens.

Do you provide the food?

We offer personally curated catering — put together by hand for your occasion, never an off-the-shelf grazing box. It’s an optional add-on, arranged on request and quoted separately, so you’re just as welcome to bring your own.
