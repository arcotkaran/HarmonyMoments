Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much does a picnic cost?

The Intimate (for two) starts from $250, and The Celebration (4–6 guests) starts from $350. At-Home Hosting is quoted on enquiry. Optional add-ons like personally curated catering, fresh florals, cakes and signage can be added to any setup — just ask and we’ll tailor a quote to your occasion.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking 1–2 weeks ahead, especially for weekend and sunset times. Shorter notice is often possible too, depending on availability — so if you’re planning something last-minute, just message us and we’ll do our best to make it work.

What’s included in a setup?

Every experience includes a beautifully styled low table, cushions, candles and styling details, set up before you arrive and packed down afterwards — so all you do is turn up and enjoy. Fresh florals and grazing are available as add-ons.

Do you provide food or catering?

Yes — and this is where we’re a little different. Rather than a standard grazing box or cheese platter, our catering is personally curated to your tastes. Tell us what you love and any dietary needs, and we’ll design a menu around you. It’s an optional add-on — and you’re always welcome to bring your own food and drinks too.

Where in Sydney do you set up?

We style picnics across Sydney — parks, gardens and waterfront spots — as well as at-home in your backyard or living space. Not sure where to go? Tell us your occasion and we’ll suggest the perfect location.

What happens if it rains?

If the weather turns, we’ll work with you to reschedule to another date or move your setup to a covered or at-home space. We’ll always be in touch ahead of time to make a plan.

Can I customise the styling or theme?

Absolutely — every setup is tailored to you. Share your colours, occasion and any personal touches, and we’ll design the setting around them.
